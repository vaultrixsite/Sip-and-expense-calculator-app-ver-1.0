About FinWise Toolkit

FinWise is a lightweight, privacy-focused financial utility designed to help individuals calculate systematic investment returns (SIP) and maintain transparent daily expense tracking.

All calculations and expense data are processed 100% locally in your browser context.

Privacy Policy
